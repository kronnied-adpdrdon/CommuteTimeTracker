## Full description (4,000 characters max)

How long does your commute really take? MYCE shows you, one trip at a time.

Let it start and stop by itself, or tap Start when you leave and Stop when you arrive. The app times the journey and measures the distance with your phone's GPS, even with the screen off. Over a week it builds a clear picture of where your time goes.

STARTS AND STOPS BY ITSELF
• Leave Home or the Office during your commute hours and the trip starts on its own. It saves itself when you reach the other place
• Errands are ignored, and nothing is watched outside the hours you choose
• A notification shows when a trip starts, with a "Not a commute" button
• Prefer to stay in control? Tap Start and Stop yourself instead

ONE TAP, EVEN FROM THE HOME SCREEN
• Start and stop trips from the app, from the tracking notification, or from the home-screen widget, without opening the app
• Live timer and distance while you travel
• Works with the screen locked

SET HOME AND OFFICE BY ADDRESS
• Type an address and pick the match, or use your current location
• Trips are labelled "To work" or "To home" automatically

GENTLE REMINDERS, ALL ON YOUR PHONE
• A nudge before you usually leave, a Sunday summary of your week, and a "forgot to track?" check
• Switch each one on or off, and choose the evening time

SEE YOUR WEEK AT A GLANCE
• This week's total commute time and your average per day
• How this week compares with the last one
• Your recent commutes, labelled "To work" or "To home" once you save your Home and Office

HISTORY YOU CAN TRUST
• The last two weeks of trips, grouped by day with daily totals
• Fix a trip's times, distance or direction, or delete it
• GPS glitches are filtered out, so a phone waiting at a traffic signal doesn't invent kilometres

PRO: FREE FOR YOUR FIRST MONTH, THEN A ONE-TIME UPGRADE
• Reports for any date range, including trips older than two weeks
• Totals, averages, and to-work vs to-home breakdowns
• Export as PDF or CSV to share, keep for your records, or attach to a travel claim
• A monthly commute recap and a heads-up before your slower weekdays
• Everything in Pro is free for your first 30 days
• Then a single one-time purchase through Google Play, no subscription. It restores automatically on any phone with the same Google account

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no sign-up, no login
• Your trips stay on your phone. Sharing rough commute times to help improve MYCE is optional and off unless you turn it on
• A notification always shows while a trip is being recorded
• No ads

ALSO
• Report a bug or send feedback with screenshots from Settings
• Premium light and dark themes
• Android's own backup can bring your trips back on a new phone

MYCE is built for people who travel to work: by car, bike, bus, metro or on foot.
