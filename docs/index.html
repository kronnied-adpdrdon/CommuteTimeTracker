Questions

Good to know

When can I download MYCE?

Soon. MYCE is in a closed test on Google Play with a group of testers right now, which Google requires before an app can launch publicly. Once that's done, it goes live on Google Play for everyone, and the link will appear right here.

How does automatic start and stop know it's a commute?

You save your Home and your Office. Your phone then watches for you crossing a circle around each one, reaching 150 metres out by default (you can make it bigger or smaller). Leaving one starts a trip, at any time of day. The trip is saved only when you arrive at the other place.

A detour to the shop that brings you back home is thrown away, and so is anything shorter than five minutes. If a trip starts when you weren't commuting, tap "Not a commute" on the notification.

Will it drain my battery?

While you're not travelling, automatic mode relies on Android's own location circles, the same system your phone already runs for other apps, rather than keeping GPS on. GPS runs only while a trip is being recorded, and a notification is always showing at that time. As with any app that uses GPS, battery use goes up during the trip itself.

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login. Your Google account already takes care of the two things an account would normally do: Google Play remembers that you bought Pro, and Android's backup can restore your trips on a new phone.

What leaves my phone?

Your trips don't, unless you choose to share. When you search for an address, the text you type is sent to an address lookup service to find the match. If you turn on "Share commute times", rough details of each trip are sent without your name or exact locations: the start time rounded to 15 minutes, how long it took, and areas about 5 km across. You can turn it off any time, and doing that deletes what was shared.

The Privacy Policy lists everything.

Is my data sold?

Your own data never is. If you turn on sharing, MYCE may publish or sell statistics made from many people's shared commute times, such as average commute times by area and hour. Those numbers always combine at least 20 people and contain nothing that could identify anyone. Individual trips, your random ID, and anything linked to your phone are never sold or shared, and nothing is used for advertising.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. MYCE is for Android phones running Android 8 or newer, through Google Play.

How do I delete my data?

Delete trips one at a time in History, or all at once in Settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything on the phone. The Delete your data page covers shared data too.
