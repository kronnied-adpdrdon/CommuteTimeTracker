# Privacy Policy — Commute Time Tracker

Last updated: [DATE]

Commute Time Tracker ("the app") is published by [YOUR LEGAL NAME] ("we"). Contact: [SUPPORT EMAIL].

**In short:** the app has no accounts and no servers of ours. Your trips stay on your phone.

## What the app collects

- **Precise location**, only while you have started a commute. Tracking begins when you tap Start (in the app or on its home-screen widget) and ends when you tap Stop (in the app, on the widget, or in the notification). While tracking, the app shows a persistent notification and may keep collecting location with the screen off or the app closed. It does not collect location at any other time. Location comes from your phone's own location services (Google Play services).
- **Trip data**: for each commute you record, the start and end times, duration, distance, the start and end location, and whether it was a trip to work or home. The full route is used only to measure distance while the trip is running; it is not saved.
- **Saved places**: the Home and Office locations you choose to set, with the address you picked.
- **Address search**: when you type an address and press Search, the text you typed is sent over HTTPS to your phone's built-in address lookup (provided by Google on most phones) and, if that finds nothing, to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). Only the typed text is sent, only when you press Search. We do not receive it. Those services have their own privacy policies.
- **Reminders**: if you leave notifications on, the app schedules reminders on your phone (for example a weekly summary) using your saved trips. Nothing is sent anywhere for this.
- **Bug reports and feedback**: if you choose Report a bug or Send feedback, the app opens your own email app with your message, any screenshots you add and, for bug reports, a diagnostics file attached. You can read everything before pressing Send, and nothing is sent unless you do. The diagnostics file lists your phone model, Android version, app version, the state of location and notification permissions and recent app events. It does not contain your locations, addresses or trips.
- **Purchase status**: whether you have bought Pro. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your card or payment details.

## Where it is stored

All of the above is stored on your phone. We do not run servers and do not receive your trips or location.

If Android backup is switched on for your Google account, Android may include the app's data in your device backup, stored by Google, so it can be restored on a reinstall or a new phone. This is controlled in your phone's settings, not by the app.

Address text you search for is processed by the lookup services named above; we do not keep it. Emails you send us reach us through your email provider; we use them only to respond and to fix problems.

Purchases are processed by Google Play. [IF REVENUECAT IS USED: Purchase validation is handled by RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app identifier and your purchase receipt, not your trips or location.]

## How it is used

To time your commutes and measure distance, label trips to work or home, show your history and weekly summaries, send the reminders you turn on, generate the reports you ask for, and unlock Pro. Emails you send are used to answer you and to fix bugs. We do not sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it with advertisers.

## How long it is kept

Trips stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app. History shows the last two weeks; older trips are kept so they can be included in reports.

## Deleting your data

- Delete individual trips in the app (History), or all trips at once (Settings → Delete all trips).
- Delete everything by clearing the app's storage (Settings → Apps → Commute Time Tracker → Storage → Clear storage) or by uninstalling the app.
- Device backups stored by Google are managed in your Google account and phone backup settings.

## Your choices

You can deny or revoke location permission at any time in your phone's settings; tracking will not work without it.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
