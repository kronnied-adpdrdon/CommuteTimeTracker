# Delete your Commute Time Tracker account

You can delete your account and data in either way below.

## In the app
1. Open **Profile**.
2. Tap **Delete account** and confirm.

## Without the app
Email [SUPPORT EMAIL] from the address on your profile, or tell us the phone number registered to the account, with the subject "Delete my account". We will delete it within [30] days and confirm by reply.

## What is deleted
Your sign-in account (phone number), all recorded trips (times, distances, and start and end locations), saved Home and Office locations, and profile details (name, email). This cannot be undone.

## What is not deleted
Purchase records held by Google Play, which are governed by Google's policies.
