# Delete your data — MYCE

MYCE (Android app, published by Kaustav Dutta) has no accounts. Your trips, Home, Office and settings are stored on your phone, so you delete them in the app. Nothing below needs an email or a form.

## Steps

1. **Commute times you shared.** In MYCE, open **Settings → Help improve MYCE** and turn off **Share commute times**. Everything shared from your phone is hidden from us as soon as the request reaches us (straight away, or the next time your phone is online) and removed within 48 hours.
2. **App-usage statistics.** In the same place, turn off **Share how you use the app**. Collection stops and the random app-instance ID is reset.
3. **Trips.** Delete one trip in **History**, or all of them in **Settings → Your Data → Delete all trips**.
4. **Home and Office.** Clear them in **Settings → Home and Office**.
5. **Everything on the phone at once.** Clear the app's storage (phone **Settings → Apps → MYCE → Storage → Clear storage**), or uninstall the app.

## What is deleted and what is kept

| Data | Deleted | Kept |
|---|---|---|
| Trips, Home, Office, settings (on your phone) | When you delete them, clear storage or uninstall | Nothing is kept by us; it never left your phone |
| Shared commute times | Within 48 hours of turning sharing off | Nothing. If you uninstall without turning it off, the records can no longer be linked to your phone and are deleted 24 months after they were shared |
| App-usage statistics | Collection stops and the ID is reset when you turn it off | Events already sent are not linked to your name, email or phone number, and are deleted after 14 months (Google Analytics) and 24 months (our copy) |
| Phone backups | Managed in your Google account and phone backup settings | — |
